Say and don't say

Say

  • IMD's cyclone bulletins are read live from its own archive.
  • Wind and sea limits come from IMD's criteria for warning fishermen.
  • Thunderstorms are a forecast, and DHRUVA says so.
  • Tide times are a model estimate, not for navigation.
  • The route is a straight-line check against wave limits and storms.
  • The fish-decline answer compares the sea, not the catch.

Don't say

  • "DHRUVA detects lightning". There is no lightning observation feed.
  • "Safest route navigation". It is a reference line, not a route planner.
  • "All safety limits are official". Small-boat wave limits are still provisional.
  • "Fish declined because of…". The answer only says "a possible reason".
  • "Reviewed by native speakers", until the review is done.
  • "Connected to Nabhmitra". It is still simulated.
